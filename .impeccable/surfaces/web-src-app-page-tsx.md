# Surface brief — Storefront home (and shared storefront world)

Scope: storefront home `/` plus the shared world every storefront, account and admin surface inherits. Mode: Persuade (home); shop/product/checkout lean Operate inside the same world; admin is Operate with brand in details.

Audience/job: Gen-Z gifters and self-buyers from Instagram/WhatsApp on phones; must grasp "handmade crochet, buy or commission" within one viewport, then shop or start a custom Work Order. Proof: real products (samples labelled until photos arrive), lead times, fiber/size facts. No invented reviews, counts or press.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The name taken literally — a tiny cozy handmade factory where yarn goes in and plushies come out; the production line IS the order status. Refuses the pastel boutique: photo carousel, category circles, testimonial slider.

OWN-WORLD: Cream paper ground, chocolate-brown ink, kraft job tickets with punched holes and perforations, rubber-stamp statuses (named shapes, never colour-only), butter-yellow tape as the one loud accent, dusty rose reserved for the single live element: the yarn thread and active state. Chunky rounded display voice against a clean sans; tabular numerals for batch/order numbers.

STORY: See yarn become a thing (thread unspools from a ball, hook stitches sections in), learn each piece is made by one pair of hands with a real lead time, browse the Shelf, realise you can order a Work Order for your own idea or tweak any piece, and track it station by station.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 55%: giant display line "Yarn in. Fuzzballs out." with the sub-line on handmade + made-to-order, primary "Shop the shelf" and secondary "Start a work order". Right 45%: large SVG yarn ball with the rose thread leaving it, curving under the headline and exiting down the page as the conveyor line; a crochet hook hooked into the thread; a kraft ticket pinned to the ball ("Batch #001 · one of one"). Marquee tape strip at the bottom edge.

FORM: Candidate 7 of 7, The Factory Floor (literal name world), seed 9df3606a. Raises: thread state by stroke (solid done / dashed in progress / loose end waiting); rose reserved for the live thing; addresses everywhere (Station, Batch #, WO-###); giant cropped words as category doors; statuses as named stamps. Signature interaction: the scroll-drawn thread that runs through numbered stations, stitched in by the hook. Code-led.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
